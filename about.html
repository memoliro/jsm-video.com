About JSM-Video.com

A free, private video & audio utility that runs entirely in your browser.

JSM-Video.com lets you convert between popular formats (MP4, MOV, WebM, MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC…), rotate or flip video, remove audio, resize, and compress — without ever uploading a single byte to a server.

100% private
Powered by ffmpeg.wasm and native browser APIs. Your files stay on your device.
No account, no watermark, no limits (beyond browser memory)
Best results under ~500 MB. Hard practical limit around 1 GB.
Hardware acceleration when possible
For some MP4→WebM paths we use the GPU via MediaRecorder for faster results.

Open source building blocks

We rely on the excellent open-source project ffmpeg.wasm and modern web standards (WebAssembly, Web Codecs, Canvas, MediaRecorder).

Support

The site is free forever and supported by optional ads (after consent). If you find it useful, sharing it with others is the best thanks.
