Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. 100% local processing with ffmpeg.wasm. No upload endpoint exists. Your video never leaves your device. Perfect for private files.

Is it free? Any watermark?

Free forever, no watermark, no account. We are supported by ads. Advertising and analytics remain disabled until you click Accept.

What is the max file size?

Best under 500 MB. Hard limit 1 GB for browser stability. For larger files, use Handbrake or Shutter Encoder desktop tools.

Which formats?

Video: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, FLV, WMV, M4V, 3GP, MPG, TS, MTS, MXF, GIF. Audio: MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG, FLAC, M4A, WMA, OPUS, AIFF, ALAC. Plus rotate, flip, compress, mute.

Can I trim or cut parts out of a video?

Yes — switch to the ✂️ Trim tab. Drag the blue handles on the filmstrip timeline (or type exact times) to select a part, press Delete to cut it out, repeat for more parts, then Save. Fast cut is instant (keyframe-snapped); Precise cut re-encodes frame-exact to MP4. Private, free, no watermark.

Why is first load slow?

Engine is ~25 MB WASM, cached after first load. Second conversion starts instantly. We show progress bar.
