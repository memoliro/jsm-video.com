We’d love to hear feedback, bug reports, or feature ideas.

Because this is a pure client-side tool, the fastest way to report issues is via the repository (once public) or by opening an issue describing:

  • Browser + version
  • File type & approximate size
  • What you tried (format, rotate, mute…)
  • Any console error messages

For privacy or legal questions related to the site itself, you can also reach out through the domain’s standard contact channels once configured.
