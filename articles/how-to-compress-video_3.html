How to compress video to under 25MB

Need to send a large video but hit the 25MB limit? Learn how to compress video privately to 25MB, 16MB, or 8MB without ugly quality loss.

Email allows 25MB, WhatsApp 16MB, Discord free 8MB. Your 162MB video won't send. You need to compress, not just zip - zip does almost nothing for video.

What actually makes video smaller?

Three levers:

  1. Resolution: 4K to 1080p saves 60-75%
  2. CRF (quality factor): CRF 23 is balanced, CRF 28 is much smaller with slight softness
  3. Audio: removing or lowering bitrate saves 5-15%

Target sizes guide

  • Under 25MB for Gmail: 1080p, CRF 26-28, keep audio at 128k. A 2-minute video usually fits.
  • Under 16MB for WhatsApp: 720p, CRF 28, audio 96k. WhatsApp will recompress anyway, so don't over-quality.
  • Under 8MB for Discord: 720p or 480p, CRF 28, remove audio if not needed.

Common mistakes

  • Zipping MP4 does nothing - video is already compressed. Re-encode with H.264 instead.
  • Don't upscale - converting 720p source to 1080p makes it larger and blurrier.
  • For speech-only, consider extracting to MP3 - audio file will be 5-10x smaller than video.
