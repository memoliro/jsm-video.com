Why this subtitle toolkit?

SRT to VTT, VTT to SRT

SRT is classic (SubRip), VTT is modern for HTML5 video. This tool converts with 100% timecode accuracy, preserving line breaks and handling malformed files from auto-generators.

Shift subtitle delay

Audio out of sync? Shift by milliseconds or seconds. Quick buttons for ±0.5s, ±1s, ±5s — the exact terms people search. Also stretch for FPS drift (23.976 → 25).

Merge & clean

Combine multi-part subtitles, remove hearing-impaired tags, strip HTML styling, fix overlaps. Built for creators who need clean captions for YouTube, Vimeo, and OTT.

How to use

  1. Drop .srt or .vtt files — or paste text. We auto-detect format.
  2. Edit cues if needed — click timecodes or text.
  3. Go to Shift to fix delay, Merge to combine files, Clean to strip formatting.
  4. Choose output format (SRT or VTT) and Download. All processing stays on your device.
